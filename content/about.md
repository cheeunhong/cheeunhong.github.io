I develop **efficient AI** methods for reducing the computational cost of deep vision models during training and inference. My work focuses on efficiency optimization, including **quantization**, **pruning**, and **adaptive computation**, with applications in image restoration and video generation. <span class="about-more">I am particularly interested in controllable and content-adaptive compression methods that preserve model quality while enabling practical deployment on resource-constrained devices.</span> <button type="button" class="about-more-toggle" aria-expanded="false"><span class="more-label">Read more ›</span><span class="less-label">Show less</span></button>

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Relational Machine Learning group led by [Rebekka Burkholz](https://sites.google.com/view/rebekkaburkholz/) at [CISPA](https://cispa.de/), working on efficient LLMs and multi-agent systems through quantization and pruning. I received my Ph.D. from [Seoul National University](https://cv.snu.ac.kr/), supervised by Prof. [Kyoung Mu Lee](https://cv.snu.ac.kr/index.php/kmlee/).
